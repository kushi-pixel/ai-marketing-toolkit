# AI Marketing Toolkit

## About
A project by Rocket Business Marketing to explore AI-powered marketing tools for small businesses.

## Goals
- Generate promotional video ideas for local businesses.
- Create social media captions and hooks.
- Develop marketing content in Kannada and English.
- Help small business owners attract potential customers.
- Explore practical marketing automation.

## Project Status
Planning and learning phase.

## Future Improvements
- AI-assisted content generation.
- Business-specific promotional scripts.
- Simple tools for organizing marketing campaigns.
