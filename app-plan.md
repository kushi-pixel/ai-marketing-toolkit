# AI Social Media Content Generator

## Purpose
Help Rocket Business Marketing create effective social media content for small businesses.

## Target users
- Local shop owners
- Restaurants and food businesses
- Appliance stores
- Real estate businesses
- Other small business owners

## Languages
- Kannada
- Kanglish (Kannada written using English letters)
- English

## Content outputs
- Attention-grabbing hooks
- Short-form video scripts
- Scene-by-scene video ideas
- Social media captions
- Calls to action
- Relevant hashtags

## Content principles
- Focus on attracting potential customers, not just followers.
- Use simple language that local business owners understand.
- Tailor content to the selected business type.
- Avoid unsupported promises about sales or results.

## Initial version
Start with a simple interface where users select a business type, language and content format, then generate a content draft.
